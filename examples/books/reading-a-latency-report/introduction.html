Introduction

A museum website lets visitors browse exhibitions and buy tour tickets. On a busy morning, someone reports that the site feels slow. A latency report summarizes how long requests took, but a single number can hide differences between visitors’ experiences.

First, you will work through a small set of response times and see what a high percentile tells you. Then you will compare two reports that look very different even though the website handles each type of request at the same speed.

The examples use invented data. Each chapter ends with questions you can use to check your reading.
